Delete Your KEAM Prep Account

You can request deletion of your KEAM Prep account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Click the button below to contact our support email.
  2. Send the request from your registered email address.
  3. Include your account email and state that you want your KEAM Prep account deleted.
Request Account Deletion

What data is deleted?

Once your request is verified and processed, we will delete your account and associated personal data, including account information, practice progress, bookmarks, and test history, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

How long does deletion take?

We will review your request and confirm the expected completion timeframe by email. Any data that must be retained for a legal reason will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.
